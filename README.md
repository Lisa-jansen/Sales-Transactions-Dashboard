 🧮 Sales Transactions Dashboard (Power BI)

This Power BI project analyzes online and in-store sales transactions to uncover actionable insights into business performance, customer behavior, and operational efficiency.  
The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of sales trends, order priorities, payment methods, returns, and product category performanceacross multiple countries and store types(online and instore).

📊 Project Overview

The  Sales Transactions Dashboard was built using Power BI to visualize large-scale sales data and help decision-makers identify patterns, inefficiencies, and growth opportunities.  
This dashboard serves as a single source of truth for understanding sales performance across various dimensions such as product type, region, and year.

Key business metrics include:
- Net Sales: Total revenue generated from all transactions  
- Total Shipping Cost: Overall logistics and delivery expenses  
- Total Discount: Total discount value applied to customer orders  
- Return Count: Monitoring product returns for quality and satisfaction analysis  
- Order Priority Distribution: Understanding how orders are categorized by urgency  

💡 Key Insights

1. Strong Revenue Performance — Net sales reached $59.07M, with shipping and discounts representing a small percentage of total sales, indicating strong profitability.  
2. Steady Growth (2020–2024) — Sales, shipping costs, and quantities remained stable across these years, reflecting consistent market demand.  
3. 2025 Data Drop — A noticeable decline in 2025 metrics suggests partial-year or incomplete data** rather than a business downturn.  
4. Balanced Payment Methods — Nearly equal use of Bank Transfer (33.6%), Credit Card (33.2%), and PayPal (33.1%), showing a diversified payment base.  
5. Low Return Rate — Most orders are not returned, suggesting good product quality and customer satisfaction.  
6. Product Diversity — Categories such as Furniture, Electronics, Accessories, and Stationery have similar order volumes, showing balanced product portfolio performance.  
7. Order Prioritization — Equal distribution across High, Medium, and Low priority orders may indicate efficient resource allocation or a lack of priority-driven workflows.

 
This Power BI dashboard provides valuable insights into business performance, enabling decision-makers to monitor key sales metrics, identify improvement areas, and enhance strategic planning.  
It demonstrates how data visualization can transform raw data into an interactive story that supports business growth.
